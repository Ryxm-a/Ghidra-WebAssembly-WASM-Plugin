# Support

## Something decompiles incorrectly

Open a **Bug report** and include the smallest redistributable module that reproduces the problem. Ghidra version, plugin version/commit, target language, compiler/toolchain and analyzer settings are especially useful.

## A WebAssembly proposal is missing

Open a **Feature request** and link the relevant specification/proposal. A small real or synthetic module exercising the feature makes implementation and validation much easier.

## Installation problem

Confirm that the extension release matches your Ghidra version. Ghidra extensions are version-sensitive.

## Security issue in the plugin

Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not publish confidential samples or exploit details for a host-side vulnerability before maintainers can investigate.

## General reverse-engineering questions

The issue tracker is primarily for this extension's behavior. Questions are most actionable when they demonstrate a loader, analysis, disassembly, P-code or decompiler problem in the plugin.
