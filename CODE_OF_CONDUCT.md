# Code of Conduct

Be technically rigorous and respectful.

Disagreement about implementation, specifications, reverse-engineering conclusions or compatibility is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment and discriminatory behavior are not.

When reviewing contributions, focus on reproducible evidence: specification text, minimal samples, Ghidra output, P-code, tests and benchmarks.

Maintainers may remove disruptive content or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable.
